# Business Model & Sustainability 💼

SafeRouteAI is designed to be a sustainable social enterprise. We employ a hybrid B2C/B2B model.

## 1. Revenue Streams

### A. Freemium Consumer Model (B2C)
- **Free Tier:** Access to safety heatmap, basic reporting, and emergency tools.
- **Premium Tier (₦500/month):**
  - Ad-free experience.
  - Advanced route analytics.
  - "Safe Watch" (Live monitoring by trusted contacts).
  - Offline maps download.

### B. Data Licensing (B2B/B2G)
- **Logistics Companies:** API access for delivery fleets to ensure driver safety and cargo security.
- **Real Estate:** Safety scores for property listings (e.g., "Safety Score: 9/10").
- **Government (LASG):** Aggregated, anonymized incident data to help allocate police resources and infrastructure improvements.

### C. Hyper-Local Advertising
- **Safe Zones:** Verified businesses (cafes, malls, banks) can pay to be highlighted as "Safe Havens" on the map.

## 2. Cost Structure
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Hosting, Database, Maps API (OpenStreetMap is free, but scaling requires custom tiles).
- **Development:** Ongoing app maintenance and feature updates.
- **Marketing:** Community outreach and digital campaigns.

## 4. Competitive Advantage
- **First Mover:** No dedicated safety-first navigation app exists for Lagos.
- **Hyper-Local:** Data is tailored specifically to Lagosian context (e.g., "Agbero" presence, street lighting).
- **Community Driven:** The more users we have, the more accurate our data becomes (Network Effect).
