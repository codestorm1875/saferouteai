# SafeRouteAI - Privacy Policy & Data Protection

## 🔒 Our Commitment to Privacy

SafeRouteAI is committed to protecting user privacy and complying with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 and international data protection standards.

**Last Updated**: December 12, 2025

---

## 1. Data We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates when you use the app
- **Incident Reports**: Type, description, location, severity
- **Account Information**: Username, user ID (auto-generated)
- **Saved Locations**: Home, work, and custom saved places
- **Preferences**: Risk tolerance settings

### 1.2 Automatically Collected
- **Usage Data**: Routes calculated, features used, session duration
- **Device Information**: OS version, app version, device type
- **Anonymous Analytics**: Aggregated usage patterns

### 1.3 We DO NOT Collect
❌ Real names or full identity
❌ Phone numbers or email addresses (optional for notifications)
❌ Payment information (processed by third-party)
❌ Contacts or SMS data
❌ Photos or media files

## 2. How We Use Your Data

### 2.1 Primary Uses
✅ **Safety Scoring**: Calculate real-time safety scores for routes
✅ **Heatmap Generation**: Create community safety maps
✅ **Incident Alerts**: Notify users of nearby safety concerns
✅ **Route Recommendations**: Suggest safer navigation paths
✅ **Emergency Services**: Enable SOS features

### 2.2 Analytics & Improvement
✅ Improve algorithm accuracy
✅ Identify dangerous zones
✅ Detect incident patterns
✅ Enhance user experience

### 2.3 We NEVER
❌ Sell your personal data to third parties
❌ Share individual location history with advertisers
❌ Track you when the app is closed (unless background mode enabled)
❌ Use data for purposes beyond safety

---

## 3. Data Anonymization

### Our 3-Layer Protection System

#### Layer 1: User Anonymity
- User IDs are randomly generated (e.g., `user_8f72a1`)
- No personally identifiable information required
- Pseudonymous incident reporting

#### Layer 2: Location Aggregation
- Exact GPS coordinates are rounded to 100m grids
- Individual routes are not stored beyond 7 days
- Heatmaps show aggregated data from 10+ users

#### Layer 3: Data Minimization
- We only collect what's necessary for safety
- Old data is automatically deleted after 90 days
- Users can delete all data anytime

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

### 4.1 What We Share
We only share **aggregated, anonymized data** with:

1. **Lagos State Government** (with user consent)
   - Incident heat maps (no individual data)
   - Safety trends by neighborhood
   - Emergency response coordination

2. **Emergency Services** (during SOS activation)
   - Your current location
   - Only when you press the SOS button

3. **Research Partners** (anonymized only)
   - Academic institutions studying urban safety
   - Aggregated statistics (e.g., "100 incidents in Ikeja this month")

### 4.2 We NEVER Share
❌ Individual user profiles
❌ Personal location history
❌ Specific routes you've taken
❌ Your identity or contact information

## 5. NDPR Compliance (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation)

### Our Legal Basis for Processing Data

Under NDPR 2019, we process data based on:

1. **Consent** (Article 2.1)
   - You explicitly agree to our terms when signing up
   - You can withdraw consent anytime

2. **Legitimate Interest** (Article 2.1.b)
   - Public safety is a legitimate interest
   - Data minimization applied

3. **Public Interest** (Article 2.1.e)
   - Reducing crime and improving urban safety

### Your Rights Under NDPR

You have the right to:
✅ **Access**: Request a copy of your data
✅ **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
✅ **Erasure**: Delete your account and all data
✅ **Portability**: Export your data (JSON format)
✅ **Object**: Opt-out of data processing
✅ **Complaint**: Report to NITDA (Nigeria Information Technology Development Agency)

**How to Exercise Rights**:
- In-app: Settings → Privacy → Request Data / Delete Account
- Email: privacy@saferouteai.com
- Response time: Within 7 business days
