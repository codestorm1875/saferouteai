# Privacy Policy & Data Protection 🔒

SafeRouteAI is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users. We adhere to the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)**.

## 1. Data We Collect
- **Location Data:** Real-time GPS coordinates are used *only* to display your position on the map and calculate routes. This data is **not** stored historically on our servers.
- **Incident Reports:** Anonymized data regarding safety incidents (type, location, time).
- **User Profile:** Minimal info (Username, Email) for account management.

## 2. How We Use Data
- **Service Delivery:** To provide safety alerts and navigation.
- **Community Safety:** Aggregated incident data creates the safety heatmap.
- **Improvement:** Anonymized usage stats help us improve app performance.

## 3. Data Sharing
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** We do not sell your personal location history to advertisers.
- **Emergency Services:** In an SOS event, your location is shared *only* with your designated emergency contacts and (in future) emergency services, upon your explicit action.

## 4. User Rights
- **Right to Access:** You can request a copy of your data.
- **Right to Delete:** You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.
- **Opt-Out:** You can disable location services, though this limits app functionality.

## 5. Security Measures
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted via TLS/SSL.
- **Anonymization:** Incident reports are stripped of user identifiers before being added to the public dataset.
