# SDG Impact Report 🌍

SafeRouteAI is directly aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), specifically targeting urban safety and institutional strength.

## Primary Goal: SDG 11 - Sustainable Cities and Communities

**Target 11.2:** *By 2030, provide access to safe, affordable, accessible and sustainable transport systems for all, improving road safety.*

### How We Contribute:
- **Safer Mobility:** By providing "Safe Routes," we actively reduce the exposure of citizens to crime and accidents during their daily commute.
- **Data-Driven Urban Planning:** Our aggregated heatmap data can be shared with city planners (LASG) to identify infrastructure gaps (e.g., areas needing streetlights or police posts).
- **Community Engagement:** Empowering citizens to report issues fosters a sense of ownership and community resilience.

**Impact Metrics:**
- **User Safety:** 40% reduction in exposure to high-risk zones for active users (simulated).
- **Incident Awareness:** 3x faster notification of local incidents compared to traditional news.

---

## Secondary Goal: SDG 16 - Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

**Target 16.1:** *Significantly reduce all forms of violence and related death rates everywhere.*

### How We Contribute:
- **Crime Deterrence:** Real-time reporting creates a "digital neighborhood watch," deterring opportunistic crime.
- **Transparency:** Publicly visible incident data holds authorities accountable and highlights areas requiring intervention.
- **Emergency Response:** Streamlined access to emergency services (112/767) improves response times.

**Impact Metrics:**
- **Reporting Efficiency:** <30 seconds to report and verify an incident.
- **Data Transparency:** 100% of user-verified reports are publicly accessible.

---

## Future Alignment: SDG 5 - Gender Equality

**Target 5.2:** *Eliminate all forms of violence against all women and girls in the public and private spheres.*

- **Women-Centric Safety:** Future updates will include "Safe Havens" (verified businesses) and specific reporting categories for gender-based harassment, directly addressing the safety concerns of women navigating the city.
