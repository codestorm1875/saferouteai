# SafeRouteAI & UN Sustainable Development Goals

## 🌍 Our Impact on the SDGs

SafeRouteAI directly contributes to achieving the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals by 2030.

---

## Primary SDG Alignment

### 🏙️ SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities
**Target 11.2**: By 2030, provide access to safe, affordable, accessible and sustainable transport systems for all, improving road safety, notably by expanding public transport, with special attention to the needs of those in vulnerable situations, women, children, persons with disabilities and older persons.

**Target 11.7**: By 2030, provide universal access to safe, inclusive and accessible, green and public spaces, in particular for women and children, older persons and persons with disabilities.

**How SafeRouteAI Contributes:**
- ✅ **Real-time Safety Information**: Empowers citizens with data to make informed decisions about their routes
- ✅ **Community-Driven Reporting**: Enables collective awareness of unsafe areas
- ✅ **Inclusive Access**: Free, accessible safety information for all Lagos residents
- ✅ **Vulnerable Groups Protection**: Helps women, children, and elderly navigate safely
- ✅ **Data-Driven Urban Planning**: Provides authorities with incident data to improve infrastructure

---

### ⚖️ SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions
**Target 16.1**: Significantly reduce all forms of violence and related death rates everywhere.

**Target 16.6**: Develop effective, accountable and transparent institutions at all levels.

**How SafeRouteAI Contributes:**
- ✅ **Violence Prevention**: Early warning system for high-risk areas
- ✅ **Transparent Data**: Open incident reporting creates accountability
- ✅ **Emergency Response**: Quick access to police and emergency services
- ✅ **Community Policing**: Bridges gap between citizens and law enforcement
- ✅ **Evidence-Based Policy**: Provides data for targeted security interventions
