# SafeRouteAI Features 📱

## Core Features

### 1. Real-Time Safety Heatmap 🗺️
- **Dynamic Visualization:** Color-coded zones overlaying the Lagos map.
  - 🟢 **Green (71-100):** Safe area. High police presence, good lighting, low historical incidents.
  - 🟡 **Yellow (31-70):** Moderate caution. Average traffic, variable lighting.
  - 🔴 **Red (0-30):** High risk. Recent incidents, poor lighting, high crime reports.
- **Live Updates:** Data refreshes every 10 seconds to reflect current conditions.

### 2. Intelligent Route Planning 🛣️
- **Safety vs. Speed:** Dual routing engine that calculates:
  - **Fastest Route:** Traditional time-optimized path.
  - **Safest Route:** Optimized for maximum safety score, avoiding danger zones.
- **Route Analytics:** Displays estimated time, distance, and aggregate safety score for each option.

### 3. Community Incident Reporting 📢
- **Quick Report:** Streamlined interface to report incidents in under 10 seconds.
- **Categories:** Robbery, Accident, Harassment, Unrest, Flooding, Fire.
- **Severity Levels:** Low, Medium, High.
- **Auto-Geolocation:** Automatically captures GPS coordinates of the report.

### 4. Live Incident Feed 📰
- **Real-Time Stream:** Scrollable feed of reports from other users.
- **Verification System:** Community upvotes/downvotes to validate reports.
- **Filtering:** Sort by time, distance, or severity.

### 5. Emergency Toolkit 🆘
- **One-Tap SOS:** Instantly triggers a loud alarm and flashes the screen.
- **Direct Dial:** Quick access to Lagos Emergency Services (112, 767).
- **Location Share:** Share live coordinates via WhatsApp or SMS.
- **Flashlight:** Integrated toggle for low-light situations.

### 6. AI-Powered Insights 🧠
- **Danger Zone Detection:** ML algorithms cluster historical data to identify persistent high-risk areas.
- **Trend Analysis:** Predictive modeling to forecast safety levels based on time of day and weather.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Flags unusual spikes in incident reports for immediate review.

## Technical Highlights
- **PWA Ready:** Installable on mobile devices.
- **Offline Mode:** Caches recent map data and safety scores.
- **Low Bandwidth:** Optimized for 3G/4G networks common in Lagos.
