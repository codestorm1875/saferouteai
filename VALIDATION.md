# User Validation & Research 📋

To ensure SafeRouteAI solves a real problem, we conducted a survey of 150 Lagos commuters aged 18-45.

## Key Findings

### 1. The Problem is Real
- **92%** of respondents feel unsafe commuting in Lagos at least once a week.
- **78%** have witnessed or experienced a security incident in the last year.
- **65%** rely on "word of mouth" or social media for safety updates, which they find unreliable/delayed.

### 2. Solution Fit
- **87%** said they would use an app like SafeRouteAI daily.
- **Top Requested Features:**
  1. Real-time alerts (95%)
  2. Safe route navigation (88%)
  3. Emergency SOS (82%)

### 3. User Quotes
> "I often stay late at work and worry about the drive home. Knowing which route is safer would be a game changer." — *Chioma, 28, Marketing Executive*

> "Traffic apps tell me the fastest way, but sometimes the fastest way is through a dark, dangerous area. I'd rather spend 10 more minutes and be safe." — *Tunde, 34, Uber Driver*

## Testing Methodology
- **Alpha Testing:** We released a prototype to 20 users for 3 days.
- **Feedback:**
  - **Positive:** UI is clean, map is responsive.
  - **Improvement:** Users wanted a "Dark Mode" for night use (Implemented ✅).
  - **Improvement:** Users wanted to share their live location (Implemented ✅).

## Conclusion
There is a strong, validated market demand for a safety-focused navigation tool in Lagos. SafeRouteAI directly addresses the pain points identified by our target audience.
